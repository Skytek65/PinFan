## Description
```
PinFan is the neon arcade companion for pinball fans who live for multiball, high scores, and the hunt for the next great cabinet.

HOME
Jump into featured cabinets, spin Arcade Roulette, catch a trivia bite, and browse Top Cabinets — your neon dashboard for the silverball life.

EXPLORE CABINETS
Search and browse thousands of pinball machines (~3,195) with ratings, makers, years, and solid-state or electro-mechanical tags — classics to modern remakes.

MY ARCADE
Build your personal floor. Switch between My Collection and Wishlist, track machines you own, jot notes, and log high scores.

CABINET DETAILS
Open any machine for artwork, technical specs, production info, and a playfield & cabinet gallery. One tap to mark Owned or Wishlisted.

OFFLINE READY
Take the catalog with you. Browse makers, years, ratings, and specs even when you’re deep in the arcade with zero signal.

Built for collectors, operators, and anyone who still hears the call of the flippers.
```
